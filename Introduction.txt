"Such a great field that has great career opportunities in the future" every content creator said.

To be honest.. all these sayings were true in some sense.

Since then, your dreams became dark. You started doubting your ability to even

Today you woke up excited, drank your cup of coffee and started thinking about your future
and the most efficient way to reach your first internship interview.
Your goal is to reach your first interview as a Backend intern.  
You found that in order to reach your goal, you need strength points.
Strength points are earned through gaining skills.

There're 6 types of skills:
1. Language (programming language)
2. DSA (data structures & algorithms)
3. Generic (ex: Soft Skills)
4. Database
5. Technology (A backend framework)
6. Extra Skills

Skills are not easily gained, you have to study and practice for some months (sometimes years) to gain a single skill.

You're trapped inside your brain cells (a map of 3 rows and 4 columns) and you start at row 1, col 1 with your bag of skills empty.
A brain cell can contain skills that you can gain,
obstacles, that wastes a lot of time, or both.
An obstacle can be passed without wasting time if some skill type that can defeat it is in your bag. 

Your goal is to reach cell at row 3 and col 4 to start your interview.
Your strength points determines whether you pass or fail the interview :)
